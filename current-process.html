How a VA packet is built today

The manual process the new tool replaces, mapped from the practice's templates, cover sheets, RFS forms, sample packets and Lorraine's answers of 19 September. Every step below is done by hand.

Updated 23 Sep 2026 Sources reference files · 19 Sep answers · visit-note example

The flow

One authorization produces one packet, sent once the visits are complete. The four amber markers are where values drift or don't reflect the visit today. Dashed boxes are steps still to be confirmed or that only apply sometimes.

  • 1
    The visit date is written twice on every pageTop and bottom must match. The earlier red-text Jane Doe sample shows the failure: page 2 reads 04/03 at the top and 4/01 at the bottom.
  • 2
    Identifiers are retyped across three documents — and already disagree in the samplesIn the Jane Doe packet the notes show last four 1234 while the matching cover sheet shows 1111. In the John Doe packet the notes show 0001 and the cover sheets 1234. Nobody did anything wrong; it is what happens when one number is typed in three places.
  • 3
    The page count is worked out by handIt has to include the cover sheet and both RFS pages, and it changes whenever a page is added.
  • 4
    The patient's answers are the same on every visitType of pain, painful activities and daily-life interference are questions for the patient, yet all 12 Jane Doe pages are pixel-identical to the blank template. Dr. DeYoe's note for John Doe describes the pain as “dull and sharp … mild numbness down the left leg”, while the Low Back Pain – Left template has Throbbing, Dull, Aching and Stiffness ticked — not Sharp or Numbness.

What actually gets filled in

Green is already printed on the form. Amber is what gets written for each packet today.

Clinical notes

Acupuncture Progress Note

1 page per visit · 20 diagnoses

Pre-printed on the template

  • Practice header, James DeYoe, LAc, HSRM / EPS provider lines, logo
  • Type-of-pain, activity and interference ticks — the same for every patient with that diagnosis today
  • Objective muscle grid, pulse and tongue, assessment
  • Prognosis and plan ticks, treatments given
  • Acupuncture point prescription — handwritten
  • Therapies, location of pain, post-treatment 1/10
  • “Good” ticked under today's response — on every template
  • Physician's signature — on all templates now in hand

Written for each packet

  • Patient name — every page
  • Visit date, top of page
  • Visit date, bottom of page — must match the top
  • Pain level at top 8/10 — as reported that visit
  • Diagnosis wording LOW BACK PAIN — left/right is not printed
  • VA authorization number VA0012345678
  • Last four 1234
  • Visit label and physician's note — first and last page only
  • “Excellent” under today's response — last page only

First page

INITIAL VISIT IN AUTHORIZATION and this note:

Discussed tx plan & no show policy w/ pt. Pt responded well to tx. Rx: conti tx plan.

Last page

Last Visit in authorization, a closing note such as the one on the John Doe sample, and Excellent ticked:

Pt responded well to tx. Pt had an excellent outcome w/ acupuncture

How the packet tool handles this (demo, updated 25 Sep). The four markers above are handled by rule: the visit date is entered once and printed top and bottom · name, VA number and last four come from one record and are checked across all three files · the page count is calculated · the patient's answers are recorded per visit, starting from the previous visit. Awaiting Poonam/Shikha approval: at the user's request the demo also lets Lorraine adjust the clinical sections that are pre-printed today (objective, pulse, tongue, assessment, prognosis, plan, treatments, point prescription, therapies, post-treatment), pre-filled from the diagnosis's scan or the previous visit, and offers the “Other ____” write-in on Type of pain that every scan prints. The RFS is filled in as the full VA Form 10-10172 on Build packet, pre-filled from the patient, authorization and practice settings; box 21 stays unsigned and flagged.
