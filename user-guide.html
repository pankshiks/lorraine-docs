Handover guide

How to use the packet tool day to day: from a new VA authorization to the cover sheet, clinical notes and RFS faxed to the VA, and how to look after the practice's settings.

Your everyday steps

Every VA authorization follows the same path. Each step below has its own section in this guide.

  1. PatientFind the patient, or add a new one.
  2. AuthorizationEnter the VA authorization and choose the diagnosis.
  3. VisitsRecord each visit; mark the last one as final.
  4. PacketBuild and preview the cover sheet, notes and RFS.
  5. SendCheck the fax number and confirm. Follow it in the Fax log.

Signing in

  • Open the program in your browser and sign in with your email address and password.
  • First time: change your password straight away with Change password at the top right.
  • Forgot your password? Click Forgot password on the sign-in page. A link arrives by email; it works once and for 30 minutes.
  • Three wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes.

Finding your way

The menu on the left is always there. The top part is for everyday work; the Admin part is for settings.

At the top of most screens, the patient's name, last four and date of birth are shown, so you always know whose record is open. The line under the title (for example Jane Doe · Low Back Pain – Left M5450 / Build packet) takes you back a step when you click it.

Dashboard

Your starting point. The boxes at the top count what needs doing; click one to see the list.

  • In progress: authorizations with visits still being recorded.
  • Nearing last visit: authorizations close to their approved number of visits.
  • Ready to send: packets built and waiting for you to confirm.
  • Failed / waiting faxes: faxes that didn't go through, or are still on their way.
  • Recently sent: the latest packets faxed.

Click any row to open that authorization. The diagnosis code shows beside each diagnosis here and on the patient, authorization and packet screens.

Patients

Find a patient

Go to Patients and type in Find a patient: a name, a VA number or the last four. Click anywhere on a row to open the patient.

Add a patient

  1. Click + Add patient.
  2. Enter first name, middle name or initial (optional), last name, date of birth, last four of the SSN, title (Mr./Ms./Mrs.) and his/her.
  3. Click Save patient. If the same name, date of birth and last four already exist, the program shows that patient first so you don't add someone twice.

The RFS prints the full legal name, including the middle name when there is one. Only the last four of the SSN is ever stored.

To change details later, open the patient and click Edit patient. The RFS and cover sheet always use the details as they are now.

Authorizations

Each VA authorization is entered once. Its visits and packet belong to it.

  1. Open the patient and click New authorization.
  2. Enter the VA authorization number (it starts with VA00), the approved number of visits (at most 12), and the period.
  3. Choose the diagnosis: start typing a name or code, for example "knee" or "M25562", and pick the right side. Each diagnosis shows its code beside its name, so check it against the VA authorization; the diagnosis locks after the first visit is recorded.
  4. Choose the office and the VA facility the packet is faxed to.
  5. Click Create authorization.

To correct something later, open the authorization and click Edit authorization. Once a packet has been sent, the authorization is locked.

For tests: choose the VA facility Practice test fax. It faxes the practice's own number, never the VA.

Recording visits

  1. Open the authorization and click Record a visit.
  2. Enter the visit date and the pain level (0–10). Answer the questions for this visit; they start from the template's usual answers, so change only what's different.
  3. Check Dr. DeYoe's note. It is filled in with the standard wording for the first, middle or final visit and can be changed for any visit.
  4. On the last visit, turn on Mark as final visit. The last page then shows "Last visit" and the final note.
  5. Click Save visit. If something is missing, the message beside the button says what.

A visit can't be dated in the future. To correct a visit, open it from the authorization and save it again.

Building a packet

When the final visit is saved, the packet can be built: a cover sheet, the clinical notes (one page per visit) and, when needed, the RFS (VA Form 10-10172).

  1. Open the authorization and click Build packet.
  2. Check the packet type and the cover sheet.
  3. Tick Include RFS only when Dr. DeYoe feels the patient needs more visits. The RFS boxes are filled in for you; check them, and type box 14 (diagnosis description) as the VA wrote it.
  4. Click Build and preview.

Preview

  • Each file has its own tab: cover sheet, clinical notes, RFS. What you see is exactly what will be faxed.
  • On the cover sheet tab you can type the total pages or the date; leave them empty to have them filled in automatically.
  • Download all 3 files saves the PDFs, for example for an HSRM upload. They are always three separate files, as the VA requires.

Reviewing and sending

  1. From the preview, go to Review and send.
  2. Check the fax number and the destination. A Blairsville packet is also sent to Atlanta automatically.
  3. Click Confirm and send. Nothing is ever faxed without this click.
  4. The result page follows each fax: Queued while SRFax is sending, then Sent or Failed.
Before you click Confirm and send: look at the fax number once more. When you're testing, it should be the practice test fax, not a VA number.

After sending: the Fax log

  • Fax log lists every fax with its status. Filter by date, destination or status.
  • The Cover, Notes and RFS links open the files exactly as they were faxed. They never change.
  • A fax failed? The reason is shown. Fix the cause if needed (for example the fax number on Practice & sending), then click Retry. Before retrying, check the SRFax website in case it went through after all.
  • Something was wrong in a sent packet? Sent packets are never edited. Use Correct & re-send: it starts a new packet linked to the old one, which you fix, preview and send again. If it doesn't open, please let us know.

Admin pages

Only administrators see these. Changes are saved in the activity log.

Diagnoses

  • All diagnosis templates with their ICD-10 codes.
  • Create new template: start from a copy of a similar diagnosis or a blank one.
  • Edit template then Save as a new version. New visits use it; sent faxes keep their pages.
  • Export JSON / Import JSON to copy a template as a file.

Practice & sending

  • Practice details printed on every document.
  • Dr. DeYoe's signature (nothing can be sent without it).
  • SRFax details; the password is never shown again.
  • VA facility fax numbers and addresses.
  • Send test fax to the practice's own number.
  • Backups: when the last one ran.

Users & activity

  • + Add user: name, email and role. They choose their own password from an emailed link.
  • Turn an account off when someone leaves.
  • The activity log: who did what, and when.

Cover sheets & RFS · Archived patients

  • Check the cover sheets and the RFS versions for each office.
  • Archived patients are kept, never deleted, and can be restored.

Good habits

  • Test with made-up patients such as Jane Doe, and the Practice test fax destination.
  • Never send screenshots of real patients to anyone, including us. To show a problem, use a made-up patient.
  • Check the fax number before every Confirm and send.
  • Keep the Mac on and awake so faxes are followed up and the nightly backup runs, and keep the backup drive plugged in.
  • Sign out when you leave the computer, and keep your password to yourself.

Getting help

  • If something looks wrong, note the screen you were on and what you clicked, and send us a photo of the message on screen, without patient details.
  • If a page looks blank or old after an update, refresh it with Cmd + Shift + R.
  • Contact Poonam and the team by your usual email.
