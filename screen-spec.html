DeYoe Wellness Acupuncture — design handoff

Packet tool screen spec

Everything needed to design the staff application: who uses it, how it flows, real reference data from the practice's own forms, and a field-level specification for each of the 21 screens.

Revision 7 · 23 Sep 2026User Lorraine, Practice ManagerDevice office Mac, desktop browser

What changed in this revision

  • 1
    One person runs the whole flowLorraine prepares, reviews and sends every packet. The separate approval hand-off is gone; the final review screen stays deliberate.
  • 2
    Three separate files per faxCover sheet, clinical notes and RFS are built, previewed and sent as separate PDFs. Every Blairsville VA send also goes to Atlanta VA automatically.
  • 3
    The patient's answers per visitType of pain, painful activities and daily-life interference are recorded at each visit, starting from the previous visit's answers.
  • 4
    Forms built from configurationThe visit screen shows exactly the options each diagnosis uses. A searchable diagnosis picker is ready for more than 20 diagnoses.
  • 6
    Visits held to what the VA approvedNo more visits than approved, and no visit dates outside the authorization period. Warnings two visits before the limit. Treatment that ends early is marked as the final visit.
  • 7
    Answers filled in automatically, always editableEvery visit starts from the previous visit's answers. The first visit of a new authorization for the same patient and the same diagnosis starts from that patient's last recorded visit; otherwise from the diagnosis defaults (or Dr. DeYoe's note). Any answer can be changed; the change carries forward. The pain level is always entered, never carried over.
  • 8
    Diagnoses set up by our team in this releaseEach diagnosis is a settings file prepared and added by our team. S16 is a read-only list; there is no on-screen form editor (S17 is not part of this project).
  • 5
    Reference data refreshed20 diagnoses (PTSD and Brain Stem Stroke removed; Headache and Cervicalgia – Left added), the new fax number, and the practice's answers of 19 September.
  • 9
    All 20 templates and ICD-10 codes received (rev 7, 23 Sep)The two replacements are Vertebrogenic Low Back Pain – Right and Fibromyalgia; the “May 30” shoulder is Pain in Shoulder – Left. ICD-10 codes added; three await confirmation, and the RFS stays off for those diagnoses until then.

Context a designer needs

  • One main user. Lorraine, the Practice Manager, prepares and sends every packet. Dr. DeYoe does not use the tool at go-live. Non-technical, busy, works from one office Mac.
  • What it replaces. Retyping a page of clinical notes per visit onto scanned templates, then faxing three files to the VA. A 12-visit packet is an afternoon today.
  • The rhythm. Visits are recorded as they happen. The packet is sent once, when the authorization's visits are complete — possibly with an earlier initial-visit send, still to be confirmed.
  • The one irreversible action. Sending a fax to the VA. Everything builds toward a calm, well-informed review before it.
  • Healthcare data. No patient details in page titles, browser tabs or lingering notifications. Automatic sign-out after inactivity.
  • Small volume. Tens of patients. Design for clarity and confidence — generous type, no dense grids.
  • Output looks like her paper. Every printed page is the practice's own scanned template with values added. Staff never type on the scan; they fill short screens and see the page as a preview.

S7

Authorization detail

All users

The hub for one authorization: its visits, its packets, its faxes.

Contains
Patient chip, VA number, diagnosis, office, VA facility (and “also sent to Atlanta VA” when Blairsville) · visit list with date, pain level and a small marker where the patient's answers changed · progress against visits approved · packets and their status.
Limits
Important Progress reads “7 of 12 approved”. At 2 visits left, a quiet warning here and on the dashboard. When all approved visits are recorded, Add visit is disabled with the reason and a link to record an extension (S8). A visit can be marked final visit before the limit when treatment ends early.
States
No visits · in progress · 2 visits left · all approved visits recorded, ready to build · ended early (final visit marked) · period ended with visits left · sent (read-only, with the three files).
S8

Edit authorization

All users

Fix a mistyped VA number or record an extension of approved visits.

Editable
VA number, facility, office, visits approved, dates.
Locked
Diagnosis, once visits exist — a different diagnosis is a new authorization. Say so next to the field.
Extensions
Raising visits approved or moving the end date asks for a short reason (“VA extension received 05/28”) and is logged. Visits approved can't go below the visits already recorded, and the period can't be shortened past a recorded visit — the message names the visit in the way.
States
Fully editable · partly locked · sent (offer S21) · changed by someone else, reload.

Speed
Same as last visit — one click when nothing but the date and pain changed. Visit 1 of a new authorization for the same patient and the same diagnosis starts from that patient's last recorded visit; otherwise from the diagnosis defaults; Dr. DeYoe's note (S10) can replace either. A one-line note says which source was used (“Continued from the last Low Back Pain – Left authorization, 05/20/2026”).
Clarity
Answers that differ from the previous visit are highlighted. A small marker shows where each answer came from — carried forward, from the doctor's note, or changed today.
Validation
Inline, on the field that's wrong. Never one summary error at the bottom.
Notes
Key The date is entered once and prints at both the top and bottom of the page — say so, because staff who know the paper will look for a second date. Clinical findings (objective, prognosis, plan, point prescription) are not on this screen: they stay as printed on Dr. DeYoe's template.
Limits
Visit number shown as “Visit 7 of 12 approved”. The screen can't be opened for a visit beyond the approved number; a date outside the period is an inline error that names the period.
States
First visit · a later visit, pre-filled · unchanged since last visit · errors (duplicate date, out of order, outside the period) · 2 visits left · last approved visit (final visit on) · limit reached.

All users

Patterns to design once

  • Patient chip — Jane Doe · 1234 · 03/14/1961 on every screen that shows patient data.
  • Status pill — draft · ready · queued · sent · failed. One vocabulary, recognisable by shape as well as colour.
  • Answer source marker — tiny and quiet: carried forward · from doctor's note · changed today.
  • File card — file name, page count, open. Used for the three files in S11–S15.
  • Destination row — facility, fax number in full, “automatic” tag where applicable, status.
  • Inline validation — on the field, as the user moves on.
  • Read-only state — sent things look settled, not like disabled forms.
  • Someone else changed this — reload notice, never a lock that strands a record.
Not in this design. A VA login or portal · inbound faxes · HSRM upload (manual; files can be downloaded) · editing clinical findings per visit · a form designer · patient self check-in · reports or an assistant · mobile app. Several of these are natural later phases; the data is shaped to allow them.
